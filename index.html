About Beyblade

Beyblade is a spinning-top battling game and anime franchise created by Takara Tomy in 1999. Players build tops (Beyblades) and launch them into an arena to knock opponents out or stop them from spinning. The series popularized battling mechanics, custom parts, and competitive formats.

Quick start: Learn the parts (layer, disc, driver), choose a playstyle (Attack/Defense/Stamina/Balance), and try simple launches to get consistent spins.
