    About Beyblade

    Beyblade is a Japanese spinning-top toy franchise. Players launch Beyblades into a stadium and battle by spinning and colliding with each other. There are four main types: Attack: Strong against opponents. Defense: Resists attacks. Stamina: Spins for a long time. Balance: Combines different abilities. Beyblade has several generations, including the original Beyblade, Metal Fight Beyblade, Beyblade Burst, and Beyblade X.
